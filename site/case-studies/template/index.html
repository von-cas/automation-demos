Reliability.

What can fail · what happens then
Failure modes and how each is handled
If this failsWhat happens
The AI gives a bad answerThe answer is checked against a schema. One retry, then a second model takes over. The run log shows which model answered.
Gmail or Calendar is downThe step goes to the dead-letter list and retries after 5 min, 30 min and 2 h. Every failure sends me a Telegram alert.
Someone floods the formTurnstile blocks bots. Each visitor gets 3 runs a day. Past the daily email cap, the email shows on the page instead.
Visitor data piles upA nightly job deletes visitor details and demo calendar events after 7 days.
