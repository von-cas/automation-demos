§ 03

Reliability.

What every demo does when something fails
AI answersChecked against a schema. One retry, then a second model takes over.
Failed stepsSaved to a dead-letter list and retried on a backoff, or by hand.
AlertsEvery failure sends me a Telegram alert. Repeats too.
Abuse limitsTurnstile on every form, 3 runs per visitor per day, and a daily email cap. Past the cap, the email shows on the page instead.
PrivacyVisitor details and demo calendar events are deleted after 7 days by a nightly job.
